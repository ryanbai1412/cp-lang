What runs here

The real CP Lang compiler and interpreter run locally in your browser via WebAssembly — nothing is sent to a server. Edit the source and standard input, run your program, inspect the generated C++17 (syntax-highlighted), or click Trace to step through execution visually.

Runs execute in a Web Worker with a five-second timeout; click Stop to cancel early. Browser recursion and memory caps are smaller than the local cpl CLI.

New to CP Lang? Start with the interactive course, keep the one-page cheat sheet handy while coding, or press Trace on any example to watch variables, outputs, and @brute(main) diffs step by step in the Trace debugger.
