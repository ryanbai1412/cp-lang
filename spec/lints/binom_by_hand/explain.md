`n` choose `k` from a factorial table and modular inverses (`fact[n] * inv(fact[k]) % P * inv(fact[n - k]) % P`) repeats the modulus, the reduction after each product and the `k > n` case at every use. `Comb<Z>(limit)` from `std.mod`, with `type Z = Mod<P>`, keeps the tables and the inverses: `c.choose(n, k)` is the binomial and is `0` when `k > n`. There is no automatic fix, because the tables and the modulus have to move into the type.
