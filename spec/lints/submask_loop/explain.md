`s = (s - 1) & m` steps `s` to the next smaller submask of `m`, so the loop `var s = m; while s > 0: ..; s = (s - 1) & m` visits every non-empty submask of `m` exactly once, largest first. Summed over all masks of `n` bits this is \(O(3^n)\). `for s in submasks(m)` (`std.math`) is the same walk at the same cost, and says what it visits; it yields `0` last, which the hand loop stops before, so add `if s > 0:` where the body must not see the empty mask. Clearing the lowest bit, `s = s & (s - 1)`, is a different walk (one bit at a time) and is not reported. There is no automatic fix, because whether the body may see `0` has to be read.
