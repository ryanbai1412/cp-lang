Fast exponentiation by squaring (square the base, halve the exponent) is in the stdlib: `pow_mod(b, e, m)` from `std.math` computes `b ** e % m` in \(O(\log e)\) with a product that cannot overflow, and with a fixed modulus `Mod<P>` makes it `Z(b) ** e`. The lint fires on a function whose loop halves its exponent and squares its base. There is no automatic fix, since the call replaces the whole function.
