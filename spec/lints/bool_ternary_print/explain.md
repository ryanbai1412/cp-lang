`print(if ok: "YES" else: "NO")` writes the answer words at every print, and a problem that wants `Yes`/`No` needs every copy changed. `yes(ok)` takes the words from `io.answers`: `YES`/`NO` by default, the judge profile's `answers`, or `with io(answers: ("Yes", "No"))` for a scope. There is no automatic fix, because which words `yes` prints depends on the judge profile; the help names the `with io(...)` that keeps the words you wrote.
