Lifting the deeper vertex by its parent until the depths match, then both together until they meet, finds the lowest common ancestor in time proportional to the depth: fine once, too slow for many queries on a path-shaped tree. A `Rooted` tree from `std.tree` (`Rooted(adj, root: r)` or `Rooted.from_parents(par)`) answers `t.lca(u, v)`, `t.dist(u, v)` and `t.ancestor(v, k)` in \(O(\log n)\). There is no automatic fix, since the tree has to be built as a `Rooted` first.
