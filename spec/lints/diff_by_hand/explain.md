Adding `x` at the start of a range and subtracting it just past the end, then taking prefix sums, is a difference array: the end index (`r` or `r + 1`?) and the extra slot are where it goes wrong. `Diff<D, T>()` from `std.prefix` is one: `d.add(l..r, x)` takes the range as a range, and `d.finish()` returns the table, in \(O(1)\) per update and \(O(n)\) to finish. There is no automatic fix, because the prefix pass that follows goes with it.
