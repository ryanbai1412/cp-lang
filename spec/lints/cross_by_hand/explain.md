Points kept as two parallel lists make every geometric formula spell both coordinates by hand, and `xs[i] * ys[j] - xs[j] * ys[i]` swaps one index on a bad night. `Pt` from `std.geometry` is a point: `p.cross(q)` is this product, `area2(poly)` sums it around a polygon (twice the signed area), and `locate`, `hull` and the other geometry read the same type. There is no automatic fix, since the points have to be built first.
