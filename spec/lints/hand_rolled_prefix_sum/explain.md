A loop that fills a prefix-sum array (`p[i + 1] = p[i] + x`, `p[i] = p[i - 1] + x`, `p[i.r] = p[i.l] + x`, or the 2D inclusion-exclusion recurrence) duplicates `Prefix(...)` in `std.prefix` (prelude). `Prefix` holds a sum for every cut, `n + 1` of them, and gives \(O(1)\) `.sum(range)` and `.cut(k)` or `.prefix(cut)` without off-by-one indexing. `cpl fix` rewrites a zero-initialized declaration, its build loop, and subsequent `p[k]` reads into `let p = Prefix(...)` and `p.cut(k)` (`p.prefix(k)` on a domain table) when `p` is not mutated again.
