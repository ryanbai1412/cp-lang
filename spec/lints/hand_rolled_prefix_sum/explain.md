A loop that fills a prefix-sum array (`p[i + 1] = p[i] + x`, `p[i] = p[i - 1] + x`, `p[i.r] = p[i.l] + x`, or the 2D inclusion-exclusion recurrence) duplicates `prefix(...)` in `std.prefix` (prelude). `Prefix` owns the extra boundary slot and gives \(O(1)\) `.sum(range)` and `.prefix(cut)` without off-by-one indexing. `cpl fix` rewrites a zero-initialized declaration, its build loop, and subsequent `p[k]` reads into `let p = prefix(...)` and `p.prefix(k)` when `p` is not mutated again.
