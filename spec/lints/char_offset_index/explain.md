`cnt[c - 'a']` is C++'s letter table: the letters become `Int` offsets, so `[0..26]Int` accepts any number and an uppercase letter or a digit silently lands on the wrong cell or traps far from the read. `domain Letter = 'a'..='z'` names the index space once: `[Letter]Int` has one cell per letter, `cnt[Letter(c)]` says which, and a character outside `'a'..='z'` traps where it enters with its value. Prints and loops then read `cnt[Letter('a')]` and `for l in Letter`.
