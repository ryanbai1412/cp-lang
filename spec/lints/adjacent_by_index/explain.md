A loop over positions that guards `i + 1 < n` (or `v != V.last!`) and then compares `a[i]` with `a[i + 1]` is walking the neighbouring pairs of `a`, with a last-position guard and a `+ 1` index that are each easy to get wrong by one. `for (x, y) in a.adjacent` (`std.seq`) is that walk: every neighbouring pair, left to right, none for a sequence of length 0 or 1. Where the position itself is needed, `v.next` is the position after `v`, a `?V` that is `None` at the end. A guard that does not lead to a read of the next position is not reported. There is no automatic fix, because the loop often uses the position as well as the pair, and that has to be read.
