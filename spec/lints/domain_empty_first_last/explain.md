`V.first` and `V.last` are `?V`: `None` when the domain is empty (R04.2.3). `V.first!` says you know it isn't. When the declared bounds let the domain be empty, as `domain V = 0..n` with `n: 0..=10` does when `n` is 0, that input makes the unwrap trap. Handle the empty case with `if let v = V.first:`, or, if the statement guarantees at least one element, declare it: `n: 1..=10`. The lint fires only when the domain's ends come from declared inputs and literals, and the declared ranges allow an empty domain.
