C++'s `priority_queue` pops the largest, so a port that wants the largest from `MinHeap` pushes `-x` and negates every pop back. Each `-` must be paired with another, and a forgotten one silently returns the smallest. `MaxHeap<T>` (`std.heap`, in the prelude) pops the largest with the same operations and costs, so `h.push(x)` and `h.pop()!` say what they mean. For a mixed order, keep `Heap` and put `desc(x)` in the entry: `h.push((desc(d), v))`. The lint fires on `h.push(-x)` when the checker types `h` as a min-heap; there is no fix, because every pop must change too.
