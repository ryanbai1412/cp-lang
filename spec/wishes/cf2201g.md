## Library

    pub fn moves<R, C>(self: (R, C), deltas: List<(Int, Int)>) -> List<(R, C)>
        // the cells self + d for each d in deltas that lie on the grid, in deltas order; O(len)

`adj4` and `adj8` are `moves` with the four side steps and the eight king steps; `moves`
names the general case so a knight, a (2, 3) leaper or any custom stencil is
one list, not a new getter or a loop over `step` with a hand-built direction
type. It sits in `std.grid` beside `step` and `ray`.
