Everything here runs in your browser on the real compiler. Nothing to install.

There are two tracks. Do them in either order, or side by side.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Read" href="/learn/read/first-program">
    Short chapters, read in order. Each one is prose with live examples you can edit and run, and ends with one problem.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Practice" href="/learn/practice">
    Small implementation problems, about the level of a Div 2 A or B. Each one is picked to teach one piece of the language.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Read

1. [Your first program](/learn/read/first-program): typed input, ranges, `let` and `var`, loops, `print`, traps.
2. [Arrays and index spaces](/learn/read/arrays-and-index-spaces): domains, 1-based input, positions and cuts, prefix sums, grids.
3. [Optionals instead of sentinels](/learn/read/optionals): `?Int`, `None`, `??`, `max=` and `min=`, a BFS without `INF`.

Coming soon: functions, structs and `match`; strings and output; collections, sorting and searching; the standard library; stress testing and the trace debugger. Until then, the [guides](/docs/guides/tour) cover them.
