Everything here runs in your browser on the real compiler. Nothing to install.

New here? Read [CP Lang in 30 minutes](/learn/intro) first: the whole language model on one page, every example live.

Then there are two tracks. Do them in either order, or side by side.

## Read

1. [Your first program](/learn/read/first-program): typed input, ranges, `let` and `var`, loops, `print`, traps.
2. [Arrays and index spaces](/learn/read/arrays-and-index-spaces): domains, 1-based input, positions and cuts, prefix sums, grids.
3. [Optionals instead of sentinels](/learn/read/optionals): `?Int`, `None`, `??`, `max=` and `min=`, a BFS without `INF`.
4. [Functions, records and match](/learn/read/functions-and-records): `fn`, `mut` parameters, records, `enum` and `match`.
5. [Strings and output](/learn/read/strings-and-output): `Str` and `Char`, building text, interpolation, `print` options.
6. [Sorting and searching](/learn/read/sorting-and-searching): `List`, `sorted`, `sort(by:)`, `first_ge` and friends, binary search on the answer.
7. [Greedy and two pointers](/learn/read/greedy-and-two-pointers): `@!multitest`, greedy after sorting, two pointers, sliding windows, prefix sums with a `Map`.
8. [The standard library](/learn/read/standard-library): `Map`, `Set`, heaps, `gcd`, `Sieve`, `Mod`, and how to find the rest.
9. [Graphs](/learn/read/graphs): vertex domains, `bfs`, `components`, `dijkstra`, `topo`, grids.
10. [Dynamic programming](/learn/read/dynamic-programming): tables over domains, `?Int` states, `min=`, 2-D tables, `Mod` counts, `@memo`.
11. [Stress testing and the trace debugger](/learn/read/stress-and-trace): `@alt`, Stress, shrinking, Trace.
