A string s of lowercase letters (1 ≤ |s| ≤ 10⁶) has period p if s[i] = s[i − p] for every i ≥ p. Print the smallest period of s. (The period need not divide |s|: `abcabcab` has period 3.)

**Input:** s. **Output:** one integer.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `abcabcab` | `3` |

<Judge id="shortest-period">

- s has period p exactly when its first |s| − p characters equal its last |s| − p, a border. The longest border gives the shortest period.
- `prefix_function(s)` gives, for each position, the longest border of the prefix ending there, in O(|s|); its last entry is the longest border of all of s. See [strings](/docs/ref/std/strings).
- [Pattern count](/learn/practice/pattern-count) uses the same machinery through `find_all`.

</Judge>
