You are given one word of lowercase letters, at most 10⁵ long. Print `YES` if it reads the same backwards, otherwise `NO`.

**Input:** the word. **Output:** `YES` or `NO`.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `level` | `YES` |

<Judge id="mirror-word">

- `Str` is indexed by `0..s.len`, one byte per character, in O(1).
- `(0..n).all(k => ...)` asks a question of every index; there is no flag variable to forget to reset.
- `print` does not take a bare `Bool`. `yes(b)` prints `YES` or `NO`, the spelling most judges want.
- A loop that sets a flag and breaks is what the [search_loop](/lints/search_loop) lint flags, and `if ok: "YES" else: "NO"` is what [bool_ternary_print](/lints/bool_ternary_print) flags.

</Judge>
