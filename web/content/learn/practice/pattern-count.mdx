You are given a text s and a pattern p, both of lowercase letters (1 ≤ |s|, |p| ≤ 10⁶). Count the positions where p occurs in s; occurrences may overlap.

**Input:** s on the first line, p on the second. **Output:** one integer.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `abababa`<br/>`aba` | `3` |

<Judge id="pattern-count">

- Comparing p at every position is O(|s|·|p|), up to 10¹² steps. `s.find_all(p)` finds every occurrence in O(|s| + |p|), overlaps included; see [strings](/docs/ref/std/strings).
- `find_all` yields positions one at a time and allocates nothing, so `.count()` counts them without building a list.
- `prefix_function` and `z_function` are there when the problem needs more than the positions.

</Judge>
