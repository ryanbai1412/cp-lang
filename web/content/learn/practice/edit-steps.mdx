Given two words s and t of lowercase letters (1 ≤ |s|, |t| ≤ 2000), print the fewest single-letter insertions, deletions and replacements that turn s into t.

**Input:** s, then t, one per line. **Output:** one number.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `kitten`<br/>`sitting` | `3` |

<Judge id="edit-steps">

- `d[i, j]` is the answer for the first i letters of s and the first j of t; `[0..=s.len, 0..=t.len]Int` includes the empty prefixes.
- The empty prefix row and column are `i + j`: only insertions or only deletions.
- The three options (delete, insert, replace or keep) are the three arguments of one `min`.
- [Dynamic programming](/learn/read/dynamic-programming) has the two-string table pattern and how `[0..=s.len, 0..=t.len]` covers the empty prefixes.

</Judge>
