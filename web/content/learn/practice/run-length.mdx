You are given a word of lowercase letters, at most 10⁵ long. Replace each maximal run of equal letters by the letter and the run's length. For `aaabcc` print `a3b1c2`.

**Input:** the word. **Output:** the encoded word.

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| `aaabcc` | `a3b1c2` |

<Judge id="run-length">

- [`s.runs()`](/docs/ref/std/seq#runs) walks each maximal stretch of equal letters as a slice of `s`: `r.first!` is its letter and `r.len` its length.
- `"{r.first!}{r.len}"` turns the letter and the count into text; integers have no `.to_str()`.
- [`"".join(...)`](/docs/ref/std/strings#join) on `[... for r in s.runs()]` glues the pieces together, and the whole scan is O(n).
- [Strings and output](/learn/read/strings-and-output) covers `Str`, `Char` and interpolation.

</Judge>
