# Friction Report: cf2229e

## 1. No point read `dp[u]` on `FillPrefix<D, T>`

- **Area:** ranges
- **Goal:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Dream:** `corpus/problems/cf2229e/dream.cp:47`
- **Port:** `corpus/problems/cf2229e/port.cp:36`

`Prefix<V, Z>.filling()` now keeps the DP over `V` (`dp.fold(pv.next!..u)`, `dp.set(u, val)`), but `print(dp[root])` is E0305 "a `FillPrefix<V, Mod<998244353>>` can't be indexed", so the port writes `dp.fold(root..=root)`. General fix: `FillPrefix[i]` as `fold(i..=i)` (one subtraction), like `Prefix`.

## 2. `.max()` traps on an empty sequence, and `max(gen)` is rejected

- **Area:** seq
- **Goal:** hard-to-get-wrong
- **Severity:** high
- **Dream:** `corpus/problems/cf2229e/dream.cp:34-36`
- **Port:** `corpus/problems/cf2229e/port.cp:24-25`

`std/seq.cp` and R07.9.3 declare `xs.max() -> ?T` (`None` when empty), and `let m: ?V = V.filter(v => v > xs[V.last!]).max()` checks clean, yet `cpl run` and the C++ both trap "`max` of an empty sequence" (IR lowers a bare `max()` to the trapping reduce, `cpl-ir/src/lower/call.rs`). The dream's generator `max(... for c in t.children(u) if ...)`, canonical per decision 53.1, is E0303 "`max` takes 2 arguments". The port spells the identity key, `.max(by: m => m)`, which goes through the std body and returns `None`. `.reduce(max)` also works but materializes the chain into a `std::vector` per vertex (1.42x vs 1.33x on the path input). General fix: lower bare `max()`/`min()` to the optional reduce everywhere (as was done for slices), and accept the free generator form.

## 3. `g.deg(u)` on an unweighted `Graph.undirected`

- **Area:** graphs
- **Goal:** coherence
- **Severity:** low
- **Port:** `corpus/problems/cf2229e/port.cp:20`

`Graph.undirected` of `(u: V, v: V)` edges is typed `[V]List<V>`, so `g.deg(u)` (lib/graphs-trees.md) is E0203 and the port writes `g[u].len`. Already filed as stdlib F1 (std-graph/undirected-unweighted-has-deg).
