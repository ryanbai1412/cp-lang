## `std.strings.period` only accepts `List<T>`, not `Str` or string slices

- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Area:** seq (`Seq` trait) + strings (`plan/STDLIB.md`)
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1909g.cp:20`
- **What happened:** `std.strings.period` requires `List<T>` rather than a `Seq` / `Str` slice, forcing the port to allocate and copy `let block = [t[min_x + i] for i in 0..delta]` (up to `5e6` characters).
- **General fix:** Parameterize `std.strings` algorithms over the `Seq` trait (implemented by `Str`, string slices, `[Pos]Char`, and `List<T>`) so `period(t[min_x..min_x + delta])` works with zero copy.

## No `common_prefix_len` / `common_suffix_len` on `Str`

- **Severity:** low
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Area:** strings (`plan/STDLIB.md`)
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1909g.cp:11-12`
- **What happened:** Computing the longest common prefix and suffix of `s` and `t` requires explicit index scans `(0..n).first(i => s[i] != t[i]) ?? n` and `(0..n).first(i => s[n - 1 - i] != t[m - 1 - i]) ?? n`.
- **General fix:** Add `common_prefix_len` and `common_suffix_len` on `Str` / `Seq`.

## No stepped range `by g` in comprehensions or top-level early `return`

- **Severity:** low
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Area:** core language
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1909g.cp:15, 23`
- **What happened:** Top-level `return` is disallowed (forcing an `else:` block indenting the entire second half of the program), and stepped ranges `g..=hi by g` are unavailable in comprehensions (forcing a manual `while` loop with `var ans = 0; var a = g`).
- **General fix:** Support top-level early `return` (exiting the current test case under `@!multitest` or `main`), and support zero-allocation stepped ranges `lo..=hi by step` in comprehensions and `for` loops.
