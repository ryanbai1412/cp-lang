# cf2066b — White Magic

The idea: with no zero the whole sequence is magical. Otherwise at most one
zero can stay, and the leftmost is the best one to keep; that subsequence is
magical exactly when every prefix minimum is at least the MEX of what follows
it. The port now reads like the dream except for the suffix MEX.

## Suffix MEX is hand-rolled (owned by containers area lane)

- **Where:** port lines 10-15:
  ```
  var seen: [0..=b.len]Bool = false
  var mex: [0..=b.len]Int = 0
  for i in (0..b.len).rev:
      if b[i] <= b.len: seen[b[i]] = true
      mex[i] = mex[i + 1]
      while seen[mex[i]]: mex[i] += 1
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** six of twelve lines maintain a MEX by hand, with a
  clamp (`b[i] <= b.len`) that is easy to forget and an amortization argument
  the reader must redo. The prefix side is one call (`scan(b, min)`); the
  suffix side has no counterpart.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, hard to get wrong (the clamp is an
  out-of-bounds write waiting to happen).
- **Dream lines that remove it:** dream line 9:
  ```
  let (low, mex) = (b.prefix_min(), b.suffix_mex())
  ```
  Today: `error[E0203]: a List<Int> has no method suffix_mex` (and
  `prefix_min` the same).
- **General fix:** `design/stdlib/seq.md` keeps suffix MEX out of `std.seq`
  as a one-problem helper, and that is right: the general piece is a
  `MexSet` container (`add`, `remove`, `mex` in O(1) amortized over a
  bounded domain) plus `scan` running from the right (`b.rev.scan(...)`
  today gives `error[E0203]: a List<Int> has no method scan`). Then
  `b.rev.scan(MexSet(0..=b.len), add).map(mex)` is the suffix MEX with no
  clamp to forget.
- **Severity:** medium.

## `scan` is a free function only (owned by sequences area lane)

- **Where:** port line 9, `let low = scan(b, min)`.
- **What felt wrong:** `scan` is not a method, so it cannot follow `.rev`,
  and `prefix_min` is not guessable from `sum`/`min`.
- **Dream lines that remove it:** dream line 9 (`b.prefix_min()`).
- **General fix:** method forms of `scan` on every sequence, so `prefix_*`
  and right-to-left scans are `b.scan(min)` and `b.rev.scan(min)`.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **Severity:** low.

## What worked

`read<[n]0..=1e9>()`, `a.find(0) ?? return print(n)`,
`a.count(0)` and a filtering comprehension over `items()` all read exactly as
the dream does (dream lines 6-8 are port lines 6-8).
