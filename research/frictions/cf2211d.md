# cf2211d — Binomial Bits

Both peel bits from the right: at `i`, subtract every set bit's contribution
`2^bit * C(cnts[bit], i)` from `b[i]`; what remains are bits first set at `i`.

## The port expands `choose` by hand for speed
- **Where:** dream `val -= (1 << bit) * comb.choose(cnts[bit], i)`; port keeps
  `w[bit] = Z(1 << bit) * comb.fact(i)` and writes
  `val -= w[bit] * comb.inv_fact(cnts[bit] - i) * ki` with `ki = comb.inv_fact(i)` hoisted.
- **What:** same answers, 14 ms against the dream's 32 ms on `--max`: `choose` does three table
  loads and two multiplications in the 29-bit inner loop, and the hoist saves a third. The port
  is a hand-derived factorial expansion of `C(c, i)` a reader must re-derive.
- **Goal it hurts:** the obvious way is the fast way.
- **General fix:** `Comb.choose` should inline to its three loads (it is a small std fn); then
  loop-invariant code motion of `inv_fact(i)` is the C++ compiler's job. Measure after.
- **Severity:** medium (2.3x on the max case).
- **Owner:** perf (codegen inlining of std calls), stdlib (`Comb`).

## The port keeps a running OR
The dream rebuilds `a` per `i`; the port keeps `seen |= val.v`, the same set. Equivalent; no gap.
