# Friction: `cf2262c`

## Temporary list concatenations and $O(n \log n)$ `sorted(...)` per candidate `d` (1.6x slowdown)

- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Measured:** `27.71 ms` vs `17.60 ms` (`1.57x`) on `gen size=25000` (`research/scoreboard.json`).
- **Cause (stdlib + codegen):** `sorted([d * i for i in 0..half] + [top - d * i for i in 0..n - half]) == a` allocates three temporary `std::vector<ll>` buffers and sorts $n$ elements per candidate `d`, plus `Comb<MOD>` uses 64-bit `Mod<10^9+7>`.
- **Fix:** narrow `Mod<P>` / `Comb<P>` to `uint32_t` and allow fusing list comprehensions / merge checks without intermediate allocations.
