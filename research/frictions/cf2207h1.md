## No mutable sub-range views

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** stdlib
- **Status:** closed by mutable slice views (decisions §123): the port now writes `x[l..=m].rotate_left(1)` and `x[m..r].reverse()`
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2207h1.cp:31-32, 36-38`
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2207h1.cp:34-39, 44-46`
- **General fix:** `x[a..b]` as a mutable view that takes `reverse()`, `rotate_left(k)` and the other in-place list algorithms, instead of a `range:` argument on each algorithm and a free `reverse(mut x, range:)` next to the method `x.reverse()`.

## No scoped temporary mutation

- **Goal it hurts:** hard to get wrong
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** syntax
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2207h1.cp:31-32, 36-38, 49`
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2207h1.cp:34-39, 44-46, 58-61`
- **General fix:** a scoped form (`with x[..].reversed: body`) that applies an invertible in-place change for the body and undoes it after. Today every query is bracketed by a do and an undo that must be kept as exact inverses by hand.
