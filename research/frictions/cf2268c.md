# Friction: `cf2268c`

## `SparseTable` uses `std::function` + nested 64-bit vectors and `Ends` allocates per iteration (2.6x slowdown, 6.2x RSS)

- **Severity:** high
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Measured:** `580.16 ms` vs `221.26 ms` (`2.62x`, RSS `6.20x`) on `gen size=25000` (`research/scoreboard.json`).
- **Cause (stdlib + layout):** `SparseTable<I, Int>(max, a)` stores `std::function<ll(ll, ll)> op` (indirect call per `fold`) and `std::vector<std::vector<ll>> rows` (8 B elements instead of flat 4 B table), and constructing `Ends([INF, INF], [-INF, -INF])` inside the inner loop emits temporary `std::vector<ll>` allocations.
- **Fix:** monomorphize `SparseTable` over its operation (no `std::function`), flatten `rows`, and emit fixed-size `[0..2]Int` literals as stack `std::array` without heap allocation.
