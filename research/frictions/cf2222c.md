# cf2222c — Median Split

Port = dream: the global median `med` must be the median of every piece, so with prefix counts
of `< med` and `<= med` a DP over odd-length cuts keeps the most pieces.

## Gaps

None. Two `prefix` tables and `fold(i..j)` give the counts, and `dp[j] max= cur + 1` over an
optional table is the editorial's recurrence. O(n^2) with `n <= 5000` is the intended bound.
