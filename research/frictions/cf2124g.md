## No `prefix_mins()` / `suffix_sums()` / `suffix_maxs()` sequence helpers
- **Where:** `cf2124g.cp` lines 13–14, 28–31, 45–46, 51 (`dream/cf2124g.cp` lines 13, 27–28, 42, 47):
  ```cp
  var pref_min: [Pos]Int = a
  for i in Pos if i != Pos.first: pref_min[i] min= pref_min[(i - 1)!]
  ...
  for d in (1..n).rev: ans[d - 1] max= ans[d]
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** Prefix minima, suffix sums (seeded from a right boundary value), and suffix maxima are each written as manual loops over `Pos` / `0..n`.
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Ideal code (`dream/cf2124g.cp` lines 13, 27–28, 47):**
  ```cp
  let pref_min = a.prefix_mins()
  lifted[i..j] = a[i..j].with_first(cap).prefix_mins()
  suff_loss[i..j] = lifted[i..j].suffix_sums(from: suff_loss[j])
  print(ans.suffix_maxs())
  ```
- **General fix:** Add `prefix_mins`, `suffix_sums`, and `suffix_maxs` in `std.seq` / `std.prefix`.
- **Severity:** low

  ```cp

  ```
- **What felt wrong:** Iterating a half-open subrange `[i, j)` of `Pos` where `j` starts at the past-the-end cut `n` drops to raw `Int` `i.ord..j` and `Pos(t)` casts.
