## Vertices and edge midpoints share one raw domain

- **Goal it hurts:** hard to get wrong
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** types
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2101f.cp:25-28, 35`
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2101f.cp:25-34, 50`
- **General fix:** A sum domain (`domain Node = V | E`) whose members index one table and support `is V` tests, plus a tree constructor that subdivides edges into it. Today the port encodes midpoints as `Node(n + e)` and tests `is_vertex` by index arithmetic.

## No per-branch layer histogram from a tree

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** stdlib
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2101f.cp:35-41`
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2101f.cp:47-52`
- **General fix:** `tree.layers(from:, avoiding:)` (BFS layers of the component of `from` once `avoiding` is cut). `Rooted` rebuilt per centre costs 5x, and `bfs` with a filtered neighbour closure allocates per visit (3.5x), so the port keeps a recursive closure that mutates a captured histogram.
