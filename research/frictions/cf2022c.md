## `@memo` accepts no enum or optional-enum key
- **Where:** the frontier DP. `@memo fn best(c: 0..=100_000, behind: ?Row)` and `(c, f: Front)` are both E0501.
- **Dream:** `@memo fn best(c: Col.Cut, f: Front)`, matching on `Flush` / `Behind(z)`.
- **Port today:** a bottom-up table `flush`/`lag[z]`, filled right to left, with the residue argument (`(n - c) % 3`) that the memo never needed.
- **Goal it hurts:** express (the recurrence becomes a table plus a fill order).
- **General fix (core):** spec 14's rule: every `Index` key, enums and `?T` of one included, gets a dense table.
- **Severity:** medium.

## No payload variant or two-label negation
- **Where:** `Front` is three bare variants and `lagging`/`behind` map them to `?Row`; `other(z)` flips a row.
- **Dream:** `enum Front: Flush, Behind(Row)` and `!z` on a two-label enum.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **General fix (core):** payload variants as memo keys; `!` on `Bool`-like two-label enums is a syntax question for the syntax owner.
- **Severity:** low.

## A `[3]T` literal heap-allocates
- **Where:** the first draft's `won(cells: [3](row: Row, col: Int))`, called per cell triple.
- **Effect:** lowers to `std::vector` built per call: 2.28x the original (11.4 ms vs 5.0 ms at n = 25,000 in the scoreboard).
- **Port today:** `won(a, b, c)` over three `vote`s.
- **Goal it hurts:** performance (the obvious fixed-size value allocates).
- **General fix (codegen):** a `[k]T` with constant `k` lowers to `std::array<T, k>`.
- **Severity:** medium.
