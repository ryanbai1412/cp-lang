# cf2147h — Maxflow GCD Coloring

## `Bitset<D>` lacks direct `for v in bitset:` iteration and `.find(pred)`
- **Where:** `cf2147h.cp`, lines 29 and 35 (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 26 and 32):
  ```cp
  for u in nbr.each():
  ...
  while let v = first(v for v in alive.each() if (adj[v] & alive).len % 2 == 1):
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** `Subset<D>` supports direct `for v in s:` iteration, while `Bitset<D>` still requires `.each()` in every `for` loop and generator (`for v in b` fails with `E0302: a for can't walk over a Bitset<V>`). Finding an element by predicate also needs a `first(v for v in b.each() if ...)` generator rather than `b.find(v => ...)`.
- **Goal it hurts:** coherence, express.
- **Ideal code (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 25–34):**
  ```cp
  for u in nbr:
  ...
  while let v = alive.find(v => (adj[v] & alive).len % 2 == 1):
  ```
- **General fix:** Implement `Seq` directly on `Bitset<D>` (or unify `Bitset<D>` and `Subset<D>` into `Bits<D>` as in the 0.3 spec) and add `.find(pred) -> ?T` on `Seq<T>`. Helps every graph/DP problem using `Bitset<D>`.
- **Severity:** medium.

## No `gomory_hu()` on `FlowGraph<V>`
- **Where:** `cf2147h.cp`, lines 7–16 (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 13–14):
  ```cp
  var fg = FlowGraph<V>()
  for e in edges: fg.add_both(e.u, e.v, e.w)
  var par: [V]V = V.first
  var g = 0
  for i in V if i != V.first:
      fg.reset()
      g = gcd(g, fg.max_flow(i, par[i]))
      let cut = fg.min_cut(i)
      for j in V if j > i and par[j] == par[i] and cut[j]:
          par[j] = i
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** Gusfield's 10-line Gomory-Hu loop is standard textbook boilerplate that contestants copy from KACTL (`gomoryHu`); `FlowGraph.reset()` made it fast and clean, but expressing "take the gcd of all Gomory-Hu tree cuts" still requires hand-writing the parent-reassignment loop.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **Ideal code (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 13–14):**
  ```cp
  let gh = FlowGraph<V>.undirected(edges).gomory_hu()
  if gh.weights.fold(0, gcd) != 1:
  ```
- **General fix:** Add `gomory_hu(mut self)` to `std.flow` returning the cut-equivalent tree edges `(u: V, v: V, w: Int)`.
- **Severity:** low.

## Domains cannot be passed directly to `print` or `.partition` without materializing `[v for v in V]`
- **Where:** `cf2147h.cp`, lines 21 and 47 (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 17 and 44):
  ```cp
  print([v for v in V])
  ...
  let (c1, c0) = [v for v in V].partition(v => v in side1)
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** `V` is already an ordered sequence of its elements, but printing all vertices or partitioning `V` by a predicate requires wrapping it in `[v for v in V]`.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, coherence.
- **Ideal code (`dream/cf2147h.cp`, lines 17 and 44):**
  ```cp
  print(V)
  ...
  let (c1, c0) = V.partition(v => v in side1)
  ```
- **General fix:** Allow `Domain` values to be used wherever a `Seq<D>` is expected (`.partition`, `print`, etc.).
- **Severity:** low.
