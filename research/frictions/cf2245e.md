# Friction: `cf2245e`

## `Dsu.groups()` allocates nested `std::vector<std::vector<int>>` buckets (1.8x slowdown)

- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Measured:** `69.92 ms` vs `38.64 ms` (`1.81x`) on `gen size=25000` (`research/scoreboard.json`).
- **Cause (stdlib cost):** `pieces.groups()` builds and returns a `std::vector<std::vector<int>>` with one heap allocation per component, where the original aggregates per-root counts in flat arrays.
- **Fix:** back `Dsu.groups()` with a flat CSR slice view (two allocations total) or provide per-component fold/count helpers.
