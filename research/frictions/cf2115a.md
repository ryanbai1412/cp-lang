# cf2115a — Gellyfish and Flaming Peony

## Missing divisor-multiple zeta transform in `std.math`

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** math
- **Status:** wontfix: the rewritten dream uses a deterministic gcd shortest path and no longer needs a zeta transform
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2115a.cp:31` (`let mult_sum = freq.mult_zeta()`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2115a.cp:33-35` writes the `O(V log V)` nested harmonic loop by hand (`for d in 1..=vmax: for m in (d..=vmax).step(d): mult_sum[d] += freq[m]`).
- **Notes:** `std.math.Sieve.mobius_table()` already replaced the hand-rolled Mobius sieve; adding a divisor/multiple zeta + Mobius transform pair to `std.math` (parallel to `std.ntt`'s SOS zeta/Mobius) would remove the remaining hand harmonic loop.

## No `.fold(op)` method on arrays / slices without an explicit identity

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** seq
- **Status:** fixed: generalized `std.algebra.fold` to `<S: Seq, T, M>` and added method syntax `xs.fold(op)` across `Seq` types (`List`, `ListView`, `[D]T`, `Range`, `Set`, `Str`); `cf2115a.cp` now writes `let g = raw.fold(gcd)`.
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2115a.cp:20` (`let g = raw.fold(gcd)`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2115a.cp:21-22` initializes `var g = raw[0]` and loops over `raw` to avoid the deprecated free `fold(gcd, raw)` form.

## No `distinct()` on sequences

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** seq
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2115a.cp:20` (`let values = a.distinct()`)
