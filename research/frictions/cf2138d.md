## A count was a list of ones, summed
- **Where:** the old line `var num_right = [1 for (_, pos) in ops if pos <= i].sum()` (also in the dream).
- **Ideal code:** `var num_right = count(pos <= i for (_, pos) in ops)`: no list per position.
- **Fix:** the port now uses it. 30 stress cases agree. Done in this commit.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, performance (an n·q list of ones over the run).
- **Owner:** idioms (done).

## 1.3x the original's time at max
- **Where:** the n·q double loop, `for i in I:` / `for (hi, pos) in ops:` calling `span_expect`, which takes `comb.inv(num_left + num_right)` on every step.
- **Measured:** 0.18 s against the original's 0.135 s at `gen.py --max` (0.20 s before the `count` fix above). Well inside the 2 s limit, but over the 1.1x bar.
- **Repro:** `cpl build corpus/problems/cf2138d/port.cp`, then time it on `gen.py --max` beside `orig.cpp`.
- **General fix:** hoist the table lookup out of `Comb.inv` (or let the port take `comb.inv` once per `(num_left + num_right)` value); worth a look by perf.
- **Severity:** low. **Owner:** perf.

The rest matches: each index's expected final value is a sum over the sorted operation thresholds of `span_expect`, with `comb.inv(num_left + num_right)` as the chance the right side moves first. The shift `raw_a[i] - 1 - i.ord` and `x - i` reduce both sequences to one frame.
