- **Source:** Codeforces 2066E (`proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2066e_orig.cpp`)
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2066e.cp`
- **Port:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2066e.cp`
- **Status:** Ported (`gate.sh cf2066e 150` passes; max-size `n = q = 200_000` diff agrees byte-for-byte in **0.91 s** vs C++ **0.73 s**, **1.25x**, down from 7.99 s / 6.11x TLE on the old sqrt algorithm).

## Measurements

- **Max-input runtime** (`n = 200_000, q = 200_000`, 500 spaced large barrels + 199,500 random barrels): **0.91 s** (CP Lang) vs **0.73 s** (C++ `g++ -O2 -std=c++20`) — **1.25x** (8.8x faster than the old sqrt port's 7.99 s).
- **Peak RSS (max input):** `44,684 KB` (CP Lang) vs `22,400 KB` (C++) — **2.00x**.
- **Emitted C++ size:** `34,038 bytes` (52% of 65,536-byte limit).

## Algorithm

Two equal barrels either expose the poison or are both safe, so every repeated barrel is safe water. Checking unique barrels lightest first with `have` safe water can only get stuck at a unique barrel `b > have` where every gap among unique barrels `>= b` exceeds `have`. At the next unique barrel `b' > b`, `have` grows by at least `b > have`, so `have` more than doubles every two steps (`O(log(total))` stuck points per query).

## Frictions (port vs dream)

### 1. No prefix-sum or range min-gap queries over a `SortedSet` / `SortedBag` (`dream/cf2066e.cp:18-34`)
- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** express and revise
- **Where:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2066e.cp` lines 19–48 (`dream/cf2066e.cp` lines 18–34).
- **What felt wrong:** Because `SortedBag` has no `.sum()` and `SortedSet` has no `.sum(..b)` or `.min_gap(b..)`, the port maintains four parallel structures (`count: [Vol]Int`, `unique: SortedSet<Int>`, `unique_sum: Fenwick<Vol, Int>`, `gaps: SegTree<Vol, Int>`) and manually updates `gaps` for both `v` and its predecessor on every insert/remove.
- **Dream (`dream/cf2066e.cp:18-34`):**
  ```cp
  struct Barrels:
      all: SortedBag<Vol> = SortedBag()
      unique: SortedSet<Vol> = SortedSet()
      ...
      fn safe_before(self, b: Vol) -> Int = (all.sum() - unique.sum(..)) + unique.sum(..b)
      ...
      if b > have and unique.min_gap(b..)! > have: return false
  ```
- **General fix:** Augmented order-statistic tree (or monoid-annotated `SortedSet`) supporting `O(log n)` prefix sums and adjacent-gap range queries.

### 2. No `iterate(from:, step:)` generator or `print(b, as: .yes_no)` (`dream/cf2066e.cp:28-35, 40-44`)
- **Severity:** low
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Where:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2066e.cp` lines 51–57, 64, 69 (`dream/cf2066e.cp` lines 28–35, 40–44).
- **What felt wrong:** Jumping over the `O(log(total))` stuck points requires a mutable cursor `var cur = unique.first; while let b = cur: ...; cur = ...` instead of `iterate(from:, step:)`.
- **General fix:** Add `iterate(from:, step:)` to `std.seq` and `.yes_no` formatting to `print`.

### Closed since previous wave
- Replacing the sqrt-bucketed `SortedBag` scan with the dream's `O(q log n log M)` stuck-point algorithm dropped max-input runtime from `7.99 s` (6.11x, TLE) to `0.91 s` (1.25x) and RSS from `70.3 MB` to `44.7 MB`.
- `SortedSet.range(r)` (`stdlib/bag.cp`) replaced manual `last_lt` / `first_gt` calls.
