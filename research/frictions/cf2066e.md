# cf2066e — Tropical Season

- **Source:** Codeforces 2066E (`corpus/problems/cf2066e/orig.cpp`)
- **Dream:** `corpus/problems/cf2066e/dream.cp`
- **Port:** `corpus/problems/cf2066e/port.cp`
- **Status:** Ported. Agrees with `orig.cpp` on both samples and 150 `gen.py` inputs (`cpl build --judge codeforces`); max input 0.70 s vs C++ 1.24 s.

## Algorithm

Two equal barrels either expose the poison or are both safe, so every repeated barrel is safe water. Checking unique barrels lightest first with `have` safe water can only get stuck at a unique barrel `b > have` where every gap among unique barrels `>= b` exceeds `have`. At the next unique barrel `b' > b`, `have` grows by at least `b > have`, so `have` more than doubles every two steps (`O(log(total))` stuck points per query).

## Frictions (port vs dream)

### 1. `SortedSet` has no `min_gap(r)` (`dream.cp:33`)
- **Where:** `port.cp:17-24`: `struct Run(lo, hi, sum, gap)` and `join`, folded by `AugSortedSet<Vol, ?Run>`.
- **What felt wrong:** the dream's `unique.min_gap(b..)` is a user monoid of ends, sum and smallest gap (8 lines). It is now one structure with no hand-kept gap tree, but every "gaps between neighbours" problem writes the same `join`.
- **Run:** `SortedSet.of([1, 4, 6]).min_gap(2..)` → `error[E0203]: SortedSet<_> has no field or method 'min_gap'`.
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **General fix:** a stock neighbour-gap monoid in `std.algebra` (ends plus `op` over adjacent differences) that `AugSortedSet` takes, so `min_gap` is a fold rather than a method.
- **Severity:** low

### 2. `SortedBag.sum()` is O(n); `SortedSet.sum(keys)` traps in `cpl run` (`dream.cp:25`)
- **Where:** `port.cp:26`: `AugSortedBag<Vol, Int>((x, y) => x + y, 0, v => Int(v))` stands in for `SortedBag<Vol>` with `all.sum()`.
- **Run:** `SortedSet.of([1, 2, 5]).sum(..3)` → `error[R0402]: 'todo' reached`. `std/bag.cp` documents `SortedBag.sum()` as O(n), since `BagNode` keeps no subtree sum.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, performance
- **General fix:** keep subtree sums in `BagNode` for additive `K` (O(1) `sum()`, O(log n) `sum(keys)`), and implement `SortedSet.sum(keys)` in the interpreter.
- **Severity:** medium

### 3. `AugSortedBag` stores one node per copy (performance)
- **Where:** `port.cp:26`. On the max input the port takes 0.70 s. The same port with `SortedBag<Vol>` plus a hand-kept `var total` takes 0.19 s. The C++ `orig.cpp` takes 1.24 s. In `perf`, about 90% of the samples are in `put` and `AugSortedBag` `split`/`merge`.
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **General fix:** counted nodes (`key`, `cnt`), as in `SortedBag`, so copies share a node and `count` is one descent.
- **Severity:** low (the port is still 0.56x the C++)

### Closed
- `iterate(from:, step:)` is in `std.seq`; the stuck-point jump is `iterate(...).any(...)`.
- `print(b, as: .yes_no)`: `yes(ok)` under `with io(answers: ("Yes", "No"))`.
- The `next_v <= max_v` check before `Vol(next_v)` is `Vol.at(...) ?? return None`.
- `count`, `total`, `unique`, `unique_sum` and `gap` (five structures synced by hand) are now `barrels` and `unique`, and `refresh_gap` is gone.
