# cf2255a — Circle Pairs

Both count, over positions of the doubled circle, the cells `hit` by a `11` or `10` pattern,
separately for odd and even positions.

## The port filtered by parity instead of striding
- **Where:** port (before this report)
  `(0..m).count(i => i % 2 == 1 and hit(i)), (0..m).count(i => i % 2 == 0 and hit(i))`;
  dream `count(hit(i) for i in 1..m by 2), count(hit(i) for i in 0..m by 2)`.
- **What:** the port walks all `2n` positions twice and drops half by `i % 2`; the dream's
  stepped range names the parity class directly (R2, R8). Both take 6 ms on `--max`.
- **Status:** fixed in the port with this report; port = dream.
- **General fix:** a lint for `range.count(i => i % k == r and ...)` suggesting `by k`.
- **Owner:** idioms; lint-fmt.
