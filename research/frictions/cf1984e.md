# cf1984e — Shuffle

The editorial: the shuffle roots the tree at a chosen vertex and
reattaches every detached subtree to it, so the surviving leaves form an
independent set of the tree, plus the new root when it has one child.
Rooting at a leaf `r`, the answer is the maximum independent set of the
rest plus `r`. The greedy "take a vertex iff none of its children is taken"
gives a maximum independent set, and rerooting runs it from every root in
O(n). The answer is the best over leaves `r`.

## A record monoid needs an explicit identity and combine
- **Where:** `cf1984e.cp`, lines 8–11:
  ```cp
  Monoid(
      (count: 0, taken: false),
      (a, b) => (count: a.count + b.count, taken: a.taken or b.taken),
  ),
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** the fold is "add the counts, or the flags", field by
  field. `Monoid.of` knows `+` and `or` one at a time, but a record of
  them does not parse (`(count: +, taken: or)` is error E0102, "expected a
  value after `+`"), so the identity and the combine are spelled out and
  the field list is written three times.
- **Goal it hurts:** express; revise (adding a field touches three places).
- **Ideal code:** `(count: +, taken: or)`.
- **General fix (algebra):** product monoids from a record of monoids,
  componentwise, so `Monoid.of((count: +, taken: or))` works.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf1984e.cp` line 9.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Area:** algebra

## `reroot` takes a positional lift that is the identity here
- **Where:** `cf1984e.cp`, line 12: `(c, _, _) => c,`
- **What felt wrong:** most rerooting DPs need no edge-dependent lift, but
  `reroot(m, lift, finish)` makes it a required positional closure with
  three parameters, and `finish` is positional too, so the call reads as
  three anonymous lambdas.
- **Goal it hurts:** express; guessable APIs.
- **Ideal code:** `reroot((count: +, taken: or), finish: s => ...)`.
- **General fix (trees):** `lift:` and `finish:` as named arguments with
  identity defaults (same fix as cf2035f's `up`).
- **Dream:** `dream/cf1984e.cp` lines 8–10.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Area:** trees

## Booleans as integers need `Int(b)`
- **Where:** `cf1984e.cp`, lines 13, 15: `Int(not s.taken)`, `Int(greedy[r].taken)`.
- **What felt wrong:** counting a flag is `b.int` in the dream; the
  conversion is a call wrapped around the whole expression.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **Ideal code:** `(not s.taken).int`, `greedy[r].taken.int`.
- **General fix (core):** `.int` on `Bool`.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf1984e.cp` lines 10, 12.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Area:** core

## No degree on the adjacency `Graph.undirected` returns
- **Where:** `cf1984e.cp`, line 15: `g[r].len == 1`.
- **What felt wrong:** `Graph<V, E>` has `deg(u)`, but
  `Graph.undirected(pairs)` of plain pairs returns a `[V]List<V>`, so
  `g.deg(r)` is a type error and the degree is the length of a neighbour
  list. Which one you get depends on the edge type.
- **Goal it hurts:** guessable APIs; coherence.
- **Ideal code:** `g.deg(r)`.
- **General fix (graph):** one graph type for both edge shapes, or
  `deg` on the adjacency list form.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf1984e.cp` line 12.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Area:** graph

## Filtered max over a domain is a list comprehension
- **Where:** `cf1984e.cp`, line 15: `[... for r in V if g[r].len == 1].max()`.
- **What felt wrong:** "the best leaf" materialises a list of n values to
  take its max; there is no `V.filter(p).max(by)`.
- **Goal it hurts:** express; costs are visible (an O(n) allocation that
  needn't exist).
- **Ideal code:** `V.filter(r => g.deg(r) == 1).max(r => ...)`.
- **General fix (seq):** lazy `filter` on domains, and `max` with a key.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf1984e.cp` line 12.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Area:** seq
