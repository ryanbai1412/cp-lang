# cf2229b — Pair Sums

Both take, per pair, the larger value, plus the largest of the smaller values.

## Reading `b` element by element beats the dream's `zip`
- **Where:** dream: `let b: [0..n]... = read()`,
  `let pairs = [(min(x, y), max(x, y)) for (x, y) in zip(a, b)]` and two generator sums; port:
  reads each `b` inside `for x in a` and keeps `total`/`low`.
- **What:** same output; the port is 6 ms, the dream 9 ms on `--max`: the dream builds `b` and
  `pairs`. The dream says the editorial; the port is a hand fold.
- **Goal it hurts:** the obvious way is the fast way.
- **General fix:** fuse a list comprehension consumed only by generator sums (no materialised
  `pairs`); that is codegen deforestation.
- **Severity:** low (1.5x on a 9 ms case).
- **Owner:** perf (codegen).
