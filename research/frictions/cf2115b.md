# cf2115b friction report

## Disjoint-sum domain `domain V = Slot + Step`
- **Where:** `dream/cf2115b.cp` lines 16, 22, 26, 55.
- **What felt wrong:** The version DAG's vertices are the disjoint union of the `n` initial slots (`Slot`) and the `q` operation results (`Step`), so the active port must pack both into `domain V = 1..=n + q` with manual `V(i.ord + 1)` and `V(n + 1 + i)` offset arithmetic.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, hard-to-get-wrong.
- **Ideal code:** `domain V = Slot + Step` with `V.of(i)` and `V.of(t)`.
- **General fix:** support disjoint-sum domains (`domain V = A + B`) with total injections from each summand.
- **Area:** core
- **Severity:** medium

## Directed graph transpose `g.transpose()`
- **Where:** `dream/cf2115b.cp` line 32.
- **What felt wrong:** Reversing a directed graph requires rebuilding a second graph from `[(v, u) for (u, v) in edges]` by hand.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **Ideal code:** `let rev = g.transpose()`.
- **General fix:** add `Graph.transpose()` in `std.graph`.
- **Area:** graphs
- **Severity:** low
