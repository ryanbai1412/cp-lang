# Friction report: cf1146h (Satanic Panic)


Ported `cf1146h` (`n <= 300` points in general position, count convex pentagons) using `std.geometry.{Pt, turn}` and `Bitset<V>`. Every 5-subset has convex hull size 5, 4, or 3, and counting interior points per CCW triangle via `(left[i, j] & left[j, k] & left[k, i]).len` yields `c5 = C(n, 5) - sum(inside) * (n - 4) / 2 + sum(C(c, 2))`.

- Gate: `proto/ports/ecnerwala/gate.sh cf1146h 150` passed (`PASS cf1146h (3 samples, 150 random cases)`).
- Tokens: port `229` vs dream `204` (`1.12x`), original `522` (`0.44x`).
- Max-input runtime (`n = 300`): `393.9 ms` CP Lang vs `148.3 ms` C++ (`2.66x`), exact output agreement. Emitted C++: `13,504 B`.

## `Bitset<V>` heap-allocates on every `&` in `(a & b & c).len` instead of using bounded-size storage or fused popcount

- **Severity:** high
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Area:** containers (`plan/STDLIB.md`) + codegen
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1146h.cp:16-19`
- **What happened:** The natural expression `(left[i, j] & left[j, k] & left[k, i]).len` inside the `O(n^3)` triple loop allocates two temporary `Bitset<V>` vectors per triangle (`~4.5M` heap allocations for `n = 300`), causing a `2.66x` runtime slowdown (`393.9 ms` vs `148.3 ms` for `std::bitset<300>`).
- **General fix:** Specialize `Bitset<D>` storage to fixed-size word arrays when `D` has a small static upper bound (e.g., `n <= 300` -> `std::array<uint64_t, 5>`), and/or fuse `(a & b & ...).len` into an allocation-free popcount loop.

## `Bitset<V>.from` does not accept a generator expression

- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Area:** containers (`plan/STDLIB.md`)
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1146h.cp:13-15`
- **What happened:** Writing `Bitset<V>.from(k for k in V if turn(pts[i], pts[j], pts[k]) > 0)` fails with `E0302: Bitset<V>.from takes a [V]Bool for a, but ... is a List<V>`. The port must materialize `[V] k => turn(...) > 0`.
- **General fix:** Allow `Bitset<D>.from` to construct directly from any iterable/generator of `D` (or from a predicate `D -> Bool`).

## No multi-clause list comprehensions or `std.math.choose` for small exact binomials

- **Severity:** medium
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Area:** core language + number theory (`plan/STDLIB.md`)
- **Dream lines:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf1146h.cp:7, 16-21`
- **What happened:** Multi-clause comprehensions `[expr for i in V, j in V.after(i), k in V.after(i) if ...]` are not supported, forcing two mutable accumulator vars (`interior_pairs`, `hull3`) and three nested `for` loops. Exact integer `choose(n, 5)` and `choose(c, 2)` are also absent from `std.math`, forcing hand-written falling-factorial formulas.
- **General fix:** Support multi-clause comprehensions (`for ... , ...`) and add exact `std.math.choose(n: Int, k: Int) -> Int`.
