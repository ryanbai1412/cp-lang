## The position bound is written three times
- **Where:** `domain Pos = 1..=4 * n`, `let a: [0..n]1..=4 * n = read()`, and the wrappers
  `Pos(x)`, `Pos(v)`, `Pos(a[i] - 1)`.
- **What:** `let a: [0..n]Pos = read()` states it once and drops the wrappers on `a`; it
  compiles today. Owed rewrite in both files.
- **Goal it hurts:** R4, R6.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** idioms.

## `ask` copies its reply
- **Where:** `let got: [0..k]Int = read()` then `got.collect()`.
- **What:** the reply is read into a table and copied to a `List`; reading `List<Int>` of length
  `k` directly would avoid the copy, but `read()` has no length-prefixed `List` form.
- **General fix:** `read<List<T>>(len: k)` or a `[k]T` that converts to `List` without a copy.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** stdlib (io).
