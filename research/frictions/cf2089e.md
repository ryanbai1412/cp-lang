# cf2089e friction

## Power series have no value type with operators

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** math
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2089e.cp:16-19, 32-33, 39, 46-56` (`type P = Poly<Z>`, `sub[v] += sub[v].integral`, `siblings[p] *= sub[v]`, `P.x * (P.x * dp[p]).deriv + carry`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2089e.cp:21-30, 56, 71-72` define `add`, `transposed_mul` and `plus_integral` over `List<Z>` and spell `x (x f)'` as a comprehension, because `std.poly` works on bare lists: `+` on `List` concatenates, and there is no `*`, `+=`, `P.x`, `P.one` or method form of `integral`/`deriv`.
- **General fix:** A `Poly<Z>` (or `Series<Z>`) value type in `std.poly` with ring operators (`+`, `-`, `*` by NTT), compound assignment, constants, and the existing free functions as methods, including the transposed product used to take steps back out of an EGF.

## No scan of a path with its predecessor

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** seq
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2089e.cp:48-49, 54-55` (`for (c, p) in tree.path(t, stop).drop_last.with_prev: if let c = c: ...`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2089e.cp:61-62, 69-70` index the path (`chain[i - 1]`, `up[i - 1]` under `if i > 0`) and drop its end with `chain[..chain.len - 1]`. `List.pairs` gives adjacent windows but skips the first element, which these walks must visit.
- **General fix:** A sequence view pairing each element with an optional predecessor (`with_prev`), and `drop_last`/`drop_first` views.

## Taking a slot out of a table

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** core
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2089e.cp:44` (`dp.take(s)`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2089e.cp:56, 65` read `dp[s]` and clear it with a separate `if let s = next: dp[s] = []` after the walk.
