## Friction 1: port and dream take the k - 1 largest minima differently

- Where: port `mins.select(n - k + 1)` then `mins[n - k + 1..n].sum()` behind `if k > 1`;
  dream `sorted(..., by: x => desc(x))` then `mins[0..k - 1].sum()`.
- What felt wrong: the port's `select` is O(n) and is why it beats the accepted C++'s sort,
  but it needs the `k > 1` guard and an index computation; the dream reads as the
  statement. The existing review says "port = dream", which is stale.
- Goal it hurts: R8 (two spellings), R2 in the port.
- Ideal code: `mins.largest(k - 1).sum()` (hypothetical std top-k, O(n) by selection).
- General fix: a `largest(k)` / `smallest(k)` on lists in std, implemented with `select`,
  so the fast spelling is the readable one.
- Severity: low.
