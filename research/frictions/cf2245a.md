# Friction: `cf2245a`

## String slicing `s[0..k]` lowers to $O(k^2)$ character concatenation (4.4x slowdown)

- **Severity:** high
- **Status:** closed
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Measured:** `50.03 ms` vs `11.37 ms` (`4.40x`) on `gen size=25000` (`research/scoreboard.json`) -> `3.80 ms` vs `3.02 ms` (`1.26x`) after O(1) `StrView`/`ListView` slice & reverse views (decisions.md §61).
- **Cause (codegen pattern):** `s[0..k].count('R')` and `s[n - k..n].count('L')` lower each slice to a loop executing `t = t + Capture(put_char(s[i]))` ($O(k^2)$ reallocations) instead of `s.substr(lo, hi - lo)` or a zero-copy `std::string_view`.
- **Fix:** lower `Str` slicing to `s.substr(lo, hi - lo)` (and fuse `.count()` on slices without copying).
