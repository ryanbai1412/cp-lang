# cf2237a — Prefix Minimum Sum

Port = dream: `a.scan(min)` and the sum of its first `i` folds for every `i`.

## Two spellings of a cut
- **Where:** `low.prefix(i)` in both files.
- **What:** decision 485's call spelling for "fold of the first `k`" on an `Int`-keyed scan is
  `.cut(k)`; `.prefix(k)` still compiles and means the same, so the corpus has two (cf2262a2 uses
  `cut`). Owed rewrite.
- **Goal it hurts:** R8.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** idioms; lint-fmt (a `cpl fix` from `.prefix(k)` to `.cut(k)` on scans).

## The dream's bounds are wider than the statement
The dream reads `n <= 2e5`, `a <= 1e9`; the statement (and port) has `n <= 100`, `a <= 1000`.
It never traps but states the wrong limits. Owner: idioms (dream refresh).
