# cf2062f — Traveling Salescat

The editorial: with `x = (a + b) / 2` and `y = (a − b) / 2`, the road
`i–j` costs `x_i + x_j + |y_i − y_j|`. A simple path pays `x` twice per
inner city and once per end, and its `y` part is twice its `y`-span less
what the two ends cut off. Sorted by `y`, every chosen city takes one role:
the lowest (`2b`, or `b` if it is also an end), a middle city (`a + b`), an
end (`a` or `b`, by side) or the highest (`2a`, or `a` if an end). The DP
over cities in `y` order tracks how many are chosen and which roles are
filled: five stages, O(n²) total.

## The five stages are bare integers
- **Where:** `cf2062f.cp`, lines 5–6, 9–19, 22: `var cost: [n + 1][5]?Int`,
  `(0, 1, 2 * b)`, `cost[k][4]`.
- **What felt wrong:** the stages are named in the editorial ("low placed",
  "one end", "two ends", "done") and the role table is the whole
  algorithm, but they can only be `0..5`; a transposed `(2, 1, ...)` is a
  silent wrong answer, and `cost[k][4]` doesn't say "done".
- **Goal it hurts:** hard to get wrong (wrong index space); express.
- **Ideal code:** `enum Stage: start, low, one_end, two_ends, done` and
  `var cost: [0..=n, Stage]?Int`.
- **General fix (core):** a fieldless `enum` usable as a domain (dense,
  `[Stage]T`, `for s in Stage`), which domains already almost are.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf2062f.cp` lines 8–14, 15, 20.
- **Severity:** high.
- **Area:** core

## No `enumerate` over a sorted list
- **Where:** `cf2062f.cp`, lines 7–8: `for i in 0..n:` then
  `let (a, b) = cities[i]`.
- **What felt wrong:** the loop needs the city and how many came before;
  `cities.enumerate()` and `.indexed` don't exist, so the loop goes by
  index and destructures.
- **Goal it hurts:** express; guessable APIs.
- **Ideal code:** `for (i, (a, b)) in cities.indexed:`.
- **General fix (seq):** an `indexed` view on every sequence yielding
  `(position, value)`.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf2062f.cp` line 16.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Area:** seq

## The role table is rebuilt per city as a list
- **Where:** `cf2062f.cp`, lines 9–19.
- **What felt wrong:** the table depends on `(a, b)`, so it is a fresh
  nine-element list per city, consumed by
  `for k in (0..=i).rev, (from, to, w) in roles: cost[k + 1][to] min= cost[k][from] + w`
  (lines 20–21; optional `+` and `min=` lift over `none`, so the
  relaxation itself reads like the editorial); a closure returning it would be clearer but
  costs a call per city, and the inner `for (from, to, w) in roles` loop
  isn't unrolled. The port is fast enough, but the readable form and the
  fast form are not obviously the same.
- **Goal it hurts:** performance and control (costs are visible).
- **Ideal code:** `let roles = (a: Int, b: Int) => [...]`.
- **General fix (codegen):** fixed-size list literals as stack arrays with
  unrolled iteration.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf2062f.cp` lines 9–14, 17.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Area:** codegen

## The port runs about 7x the original on the max input
- **Where:** `cf2062f.cp`, lines 9–21.
- **What felt wrong:** on the max input (n = 3000) the port takes 0.07 s
  against the original's 0.01 s. The relaxation loop runs over a
  nine-element list per city and `?Int` cells, where the original has
  nine straight-line `setmin`s over `int64` with an `INF` sentinel. The
  readable form is the slow one; not profiled further.
- **Goal it hurts:** performance.
- **Ideal code:** `cost[k + 1, to] min= cost[k, from] + w` compiled to
  the same straight-line code.
- **General fix (codegen):** unroll iteration over small literal lists;
  niche-encoded `?Int` so optional `min=` is one compare.
- **Dream:** `dream/cf2062f.cp` lines 9–18.
- **Severity:** medium.
- **Area:** codegen
