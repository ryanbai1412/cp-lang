## No nested-loop `first` over multi-generator comprehensions (owned by sequences area lane)
- **Where:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2129f1.cp` lines 24-29:
  ```cp
  fn add_next_triple(order: List<Int>):
      for x in order:
          for y in order:
              for z in order:
                  if x < y and y < z and try_add([x, y, z]):
                      return
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** Finding the first valid `(x, y, z)` triple requires a dedicated helper function for early `return` because multi-generator comprehensions (`[... for x in order for y in order ...]`) are not supported yet.
- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Ideal code:**
  ```cp
  first([(x, y, z) for x in order for y in order for z in order where x < y and y < z],
        t => try_add([t.0, t.1, t.2]))
  ```
- **General fix:** Support multi-generator comprehensions / lazy Cartesian iterators in `stdlib/seq.cp` (sequences area lane). (`any(bits, ...)` now works on `main` and is used in `try_add`.)
- **Dream:** `dream/cf2129f1.cp` lines 33-34.
- **Severity:** low
