# cf2187b — Shortest Statement Ever

Port = dream: four sign guesses, a bit DP over `[Bool, Bool]` states with one per-axis rule in
`step`, minimum by lexicographic `(cost, p, q)`.

## A formatter-split `if let ... and let ...`
- **Where:** `if let (nx, dx) = step(sx, ge_x, vx, bx) and let (ny, dy) = step(` broken over
  five lines.
- **What:** `cpl fmt` breaks the second call's arguments one per line, which reads worse than
  the two calls on two lines. A formatter choice, not a language gap.
- **General fix:** break at `and let` before splitting a call's arguments.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** lint-fmt.

## Gaps

No language gap; the `(w, px, py)` tuple would read better as a named record (compiles today).
