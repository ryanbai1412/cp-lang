## No cyclic domain or cyclic prefix window sum
- **Where:** `cf2089c2.cp` lines 28–38, 50, 58 (`dream/cf2089c2.cp` lines 28–35, 48, 56):
  ```cp
  fn shift_back(p: Pos, off: Int) -> Pos = Pos((p.ord + n - off % n) % n)
  let doubled = List(ways) + List(ways)
  let pref = prefix(doubled)
  ```
- **What felt wrong:** Players sit on a cycle of length `n`, so every shift and wrapped window drops out of `Pos` into `.ord` modulo arithmetic and array doubling.
- **Goal it hurts:** express, hard-to-get-wrong
- **Ideal code (`dream/cf2089c2.cp` lines 28–35, 48, 56):**
  ```cp

  let pref = prefix(ways)
  [Pos] p => total * full + pref.cyclic_sum(p - (rem - 1)..=p)
  ```
- **General fix:** Support cyclic domains (`p - k` wraps mod `n`) and `Prefix1.cyclic_sum(r)` in `std.prefix`.
- **Severity:** low
