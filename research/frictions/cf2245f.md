## Pull-form DP pays for a push-form scan

- **Goal it hurts:** performance, express
- **Severity:** low
- **Area:** stdlib
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2245f.cp:27-33`
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2245f.cp:33-39`
- **General fix:** the dream states `dp` as a memoized pull recurrence, which would rescan every candidate `k` per target; the port pushes from each `k` into `next(k)`. A way to state the recurrence once and have the push order derived (or an inverse `prev` on the run domain) would let the readable form be the fast one.
