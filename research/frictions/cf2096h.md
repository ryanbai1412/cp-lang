# cf2096h friction

## [Resolved] No public in-place Walsh-Hadamard transform (`fwht`) over `Mod<P>` in `std.ntt`

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** math
- **Status:** closed
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2096h.cp:65` (`fwht(mut hat, inverse: true)`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2096h.cp:65-72` hand-rolled the XOR butterfly and folded the `Z(full).inv` scaling into the output loop because `stdlib/ntt.cp` kept `fwht_xor` private and `Int`-only, exposing only `convolve_xor(a: List<Int>, b: List<Int>)`.
- **Resolution:** Exported generic `fwht<T>(mut a: List<T>, inverse: Bool = false)`, generic `convolve_xor<T>`, and `convolve_subset<T>` in `std.ntt` (working on `Int`, `Mod<P>`, and `Float`), and migrated `cf2096h.cp` to `fwht(mut hat, inverse: true)`.

## In-place stdlib transforms take `List<T>`, not a domain array

- **Goal it hurts:** express
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** stdlib
- **Status:** open
- **Dream:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/dream/cf2096h.cp:30,67` (`var hat: [Mask]Z = 0`, `fwht(mut hat, inverse: true)`)
- **Actual:** `proto/ports/ecnerwala/cf2096h.cp` keeps `hat` as `List.filled(full, Z(0))` indexed by raw
  `Int`, because `fwht` rejects `[Mask]Z` ("expected `List<Mod<998244353>>`").
- **General fix:** in-place sequence algorithms (`fwht`, `shuffle`, `sort`, `reverse`, ...) accept any
  contiguous `mut` sequence, `[D]T` included, so declaring the index space never costs the stdlib.
  cf2150e2 hits the same wall with `shuffle`.
