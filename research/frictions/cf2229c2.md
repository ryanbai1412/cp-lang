## 1-based output written as `i + 1`
- **Where:** `ops.push(i + 1)`, `ops.push(idx + 1)`, with `a: [0..n]...`.
- **What:** C1 reads `a` over `Pos = 1..=n` and pushes positions directly; C2 keeps `0..n` and
  shifts twice. `domain Pos = 1..=n` here too would state the output index space once. Owed
  rewrite in both files.
- **Goal it hurts:** R6.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** idioms.
