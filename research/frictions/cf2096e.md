# Friction: `cf2096e`

## `for (i, c) in s.rev.items:` re-reverses `s` on every iteration (310x slowdown)

- **Severity:** high
- **Status:** closed
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Measured:** `2078.31 ms` vs `6.70 ms` (`310.2x`) on `gen size=25000` (`research/scoreboard.json`) -> `3.52 ms` vs `3.42 ms` (`1.03x`) after O(1) `StrView`/`ListView` slice & reverse views (decisions.md §61).
- **Cause (codegen pattern):** iterating `s.rev.items` emits a loop whose condition and body both call `[](std::string s){ std::reverse(s.begin(), s.end()); return s; }(s)`, turning an $O(n)$ scan into $O(n^2)$ copies and reversals.
- **Fix:** evaluate the receiver of `.items` / `.rev` once before the loop, or lower `.rev` over `Str`/`List` to a reverse-index loop without copying.
