# Friction: `cf2255f`

## [Resolved] Radix-2 NTT + repeated bit-reversal and buffer copies in `std.poly.compose` caused 1.49x slowdown

- **Severity:** medium
- **Status:** closed
- **Goal it hurts:** performance
- **Area:** math
- **Measured:** `1277.44 ms` vs `856.14 ms` (`1.492x`) on `spec (t=1, n=2e5)` after migrating `cf2255f` from `lib.math.poly` to `std.poly` (`0e6cea42`). Phase profile on `n = 200_000`: `compose` took `822.8 ms` (85% of runtime), `pow(cat, -n, h + 1)` took `127.6 ms`, final `convolve` took `14.9 ms`.
- **Cause:** Three general overheads in `std.ntt` and `std.poly`:
  1. `std.ntt.ntt<P>` used radix-2 butterflies with a bit-reversal permutation on every call, running 68 full `524,288`-element bit-reversals inside `compose` alone (`biv_advance` and the backward pass only need frequency-domain pointwise products and adjacent-pair swaps).
  2. `ntt<P>` copied `List<Mod<P>>` (4-byte `uint32_t`) into a `List<Int>` (8-byte `int64_t`) buffer per call, doubling the L2/L3 working set on 524k transforms.
  3. `biv_advance` allocated two extra `524,288`-element `List<Mod<P>>` buffers (`tq`, `prod`) and copied `prod` back into `c` on every step of Kinoshita-Li (`17` steps).
- **Resolution:** Upgraded `std.ntt` to DIF/DIT butterflies directly on `List<Mod<P>>` (keeping 4-byte `uint32_t` storage and compile-time-constant `P` reduction), added `bit_reversed: Bool = false` to `ntt<P>` so `convolve` and `std.poly` (`compose`, `inv`, `sqrt`, `eval_all`) stay in DIF/DIT frequency order without bit-reversals, and made `biv_advance` transform `c` in place. Inlined `ntt` helpers and dispatched standard NTT primes via `if const` so `primitive_root`/`factor_big`/`rho`/`is_prime` drop out of emitted C++. `cf2255f` dropped from `1.492x` (`1277.44 ms` vs `856.14 ms`, `48,892 B`) to `1.246x` (`925.94 ms` vs `743.25 ms`, RSS `0.863x`, emitted `44,589 B` — under the 48 KB warning line).
