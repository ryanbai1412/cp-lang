# cf2222b — Parity Picks

Port = dream: odd `x` count against the even-indexed group, even `x` against the odd group, and
`best_rem` drops the largest value then every further positive one while picks remain.

## `best_rem` mutates its parameter
- **Where:** `fn best_rem(vals: List<Int>, var cnt: Int)` with `cnt -= 1` inside.
- **What:** a `var` parameter threads a counter through the loop. Taking the first pop out and
  writing `s.pop_while(x => x > 0, limit: cnt - 1)` would say "drop up to `cnt - 1` more positive
  tail values"; std has no bounded `pop_while`, so both files count down by hand.
- **Goal it hurts:** express (R7).
- **General fix:** `List.pop_while(pred, limit:)`, or `take_while` on a reversed view plus
  `truncate`.
- **Severity:** low.
- **Owner:** stdlib.
