## The parent list is read into child lists by hand
- **Where:** lines 15-18: `var kids: [V]List<V> = [V] v => []` and `for v in 2..=n: let parent: V = read(); kids[parent].push(V(v))`.
- **What felt wrong:** the statement gives a parent array, and the port rebuilds child lists with an `Int` loop and a `V(v)` conversion.
- **Goal it hurts:** express.
- **Ideal code:** `Rooted.from_parents` over a read of the parents of vertices 2..=n, then `tree.children(u)`.
- **General fix:** a parents read for a sub-range of a domain feeding `Rooted.from_parents`. This is the same shape as `read<[V]?V>(none: -1)` in yac6p2, but here the root has no entry. Owner: stdlib.
- **Severity:** low.
