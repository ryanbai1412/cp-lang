On the port side, the most common owed rewrites were frictions claiming gaps
today's language closes (`List.find`, `let _: [m]0..=0 = read()`, `.next`,
`Int(Bool)`, tuple swap, `read(none: 0)`, domain-filtered loops), separate
scalar reads where one typed tuple read holds the bounds, and trees built with
`Rooted.new` plus a separate edge binding. Several came from a stale `cpl`
binary rather than the language.

## Mechanisms that would remove the most, ranked by ports affected

1. **One spelling per idea, enforced by `cpl fmt`/lint on dreams too** (25
   dreams): one multitest marker, methods for sequence operations, one
   optional-binding form, `read<T>`. Cheap, and it makes dreams comparable.
2. **Domain-typed prefix sums at cuts in scope** (`xs.prefix_sums(at: .cuts)`;
   `prefix_sums` is not in scope today, E0201): cf2066d2, cf2084e, cf2180f1,
   cf2180f2, cf2183h, cf2187f2.
3. **One tree-input idiom** (`Rooted(read<Tree<V>>(), root: V.first)`) and one
   traversal name: cf2062e1, cf2062e2, cf2135f, cf2150f, cf2152g, cf2183f, cf2237h.
4. **Optional input from a sentinel, one spelling** (`read(none: 0)`, also for
   tuples): cf2066d2, cf2084f, cf2084g1, cf2084g2, cf2135f, cf2183e.
5. **`Max<T>`/`Min<T>` over `?T` with no identity element**: cf2062e2, cf2084f,
   cf2152g, cf2165f.
6. **`Perm<V>` with `inverse`, cycles and swap-sorting**: cf2062g, cf2084f,
   cf2187f1, cf2187f2.
