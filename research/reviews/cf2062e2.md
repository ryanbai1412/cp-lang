# cf2062e2 — The Game (Hard Version)

Score: 3/5
Dream: 3/5

The header states the win condition precisely. The body then builds every
piece by hand: the path-max ancestors, prefix and suffix maxima over
preorder, the weight buckets and the range-add Fenwick, each a loop over
`.ord` offsets. Most are reported; two are rewrites today.

## Checklist failures

- Weight as a domain read back through `.ord + 1` (lines 21-22, 43, 47,
  58), with `0` as the empty `MaxInfo.w` (lines 15, 40).
- Path-max ancestors as a hand top-down loop over `t.Pre` (lines 28-38).
  Reported (`Rooted.top_down`).
- Prefix and suffix folds by hand (lines 41-47). Reported.
- Weight buckets by hand (lines 49-50). Reported (`V.group(by:)`).
- Range add as two point adds with a bounds check (lines 70-72). Reported.
- `Rooted.new(...)` and a separate `edges` binding (lines 23-24); other
  ports use `Rooted(...)` with an inline read.
- `if winners.is_empty: print(0)` special case (lines 77-80).

- `let t = Rooted(Graph.undirected(read::<[0..n - 1](V, V)>()), V.first)`.

## Language gaps

## Dream sent back

The dream is less typed than the port: `MaxInfo.lo`/`hi` are `Int`
(the port has `?P`), `w` keeps the `0` sentinel, and the `top_down` state
`((true, st.best_v), (w[v], some(v)))` is a tuple of tuples the reader
must decode. Per `design/EXEMPLARS-PORTS.md`, the dream should say:
`MaxInfo` is a monoid over `?t.Pre` positions with weight `?(1..=n)`
(or `Max` with an argmax count), and the path-max walk returns named
fields (`is_path_max`, `prev_max`) from a named state record.
