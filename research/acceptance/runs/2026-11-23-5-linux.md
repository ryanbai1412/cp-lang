## 1. understand: PASS, 6 min
CPL is a competitive-programming language that compiles to one readable C++17 file. You write a solution about as briefly as Python, and it runs at C++ speed on the judge. Compared with C++, input constraints are written once as types (`let n: 1..=2e5 = read()`); these types validate input, choose storage widths and drive generators. Index spaces (`domain V`) and optionals (`?T`) replace raw ints and sentinels, which removes off-by-one and sentinel bugs. The CLI also has built-in sample tests, checkers, brute-vs-fast stress with shrinking, and traces. I read the intro guide at /learn/intro/.
