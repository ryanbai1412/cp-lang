**1. understand (03:45-03:50, 6 min, pass).** CPL is a competitive-programming language that compiles to one self-contained C++17 file, so you write contest solutions faster and submit them anywhere C++ is accepted. Compared with C++, statement constraints become types (`let a: [0..n]1..=1e9 = read()`), which give you input validation, integer widths, index safety and overflow proofs for free, and multitest is a single `@!multitest` line. The stress harness, checker, generator and brute live in the same file and are stripped by `cpl build`. I read https://cp-lang.org and /learn/intro/.
