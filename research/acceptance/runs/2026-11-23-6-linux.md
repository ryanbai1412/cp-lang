## 1. understand (pass)
Read the home page, /learn/intro ("CP Lang in 30 minutes"), /setup, getting started and the cheat sheet.
CPL is a contest language that compiles to one readable C++17 file you submit to any judge, so you write the editorial-level idea instead of boilerplate.
Its range types turn the statement's constraints into checked input and right-sized ints, and domains make arrays indexed by "vertex" or "position 1..n" so off-by-one and index-mixing errors become compile errors or named traps.
It ships a contest library (segment trees, DSU, graphs, Mod) plus stress testing with an in-file brute (`@alt`), shrinking and a time-travel trace, which C++ only gets through your own templates and scripts.
